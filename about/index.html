Need a Wellington Tree Surgeon?

Wellington Arborist Tree Service is a referral website for Arbormedic Tree Service Limited. All work is carried out by fully licensed and insured professionals. 

A Wellington tree surgeon (or arborist) is a person who practices arboriculture, which is the cultivation, management, and study of individual trees, shrubs, vines, and other perennial woody plants in dendrology and horticulture. Tree surgeons are knowledgeable in the use of modern tree surgery techniques and equipment.

Our tree surgeons are highly trained and experienced in tree surgery, tree felling, tree removal, stump grinding and tree pruning. We also offer a comprehensive tree report service.
